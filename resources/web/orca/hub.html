Notifications

Loading…
The hub sends printer events - print finished, paused, filament runout, an error - to a push service you pick, so your phone is told with the page closed. The PC makes the call, so this works on plain Wi-Fi with no Tailscale and no home-screen install. Topics, keys and tokens are treated as passwords: this page shows only their last four characters once saved, and they never reach the phone link.

"Open a new slicer window" starts a slicer you can see; "Open a hidden slicer" starts one without a window (find it under Slicer windows or in the tray menu). Quitting the hub stops phone access and the camera relays; the next slicer window starts the hub again.
